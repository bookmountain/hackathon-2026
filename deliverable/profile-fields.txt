UNIMAP: PROFILE FIELDS FOR PM REVIEW
Last updated: 2026-09-26

HOW TO USE
- Each card below is one Trello card.
- CHECKLIST: in the card, add a checklist, click "Add an item", and paste all the lines under CHECKLIST. Trello creates one checkbox per line.
- DESCRIPTION: paste the bullets under DESCRIPTION into the card description.
- The PM ticks the fields to keep. Unticked fields get dropped.
- [BUILT] = already in the API and tested. [IDEA] = suggestion, not built yet.
- MVP = build for the demo. Nice = only if there's time.


========================================
CARD 1: IDENTITY
========================================

CHECKLIST:
[BUILT] MVP · Display name (required, text, max 64)
[BUILT] MVP · Avatar photo (optional, JPEG/PNG/WebP upload)
[BUILT] MVP · Gender (required: Male / Female / Non-binary / Other / Prefer not to say)
[BUILT] Nice · Pronouns (optional, suggestions + custom text)
[BUILT] Nice · Age range (optional: Under 18 / 18–20 / 21–24 / 25–29 / 30+)
[BUILT] Nice · Nationality (optional, country dropdown with flags)
[BUILT] MVP · Bio (optional, max 500 characters)

DESCRIPTION:
- Display name: required, text, max 64 characters. Example: Olivia
- Avatar: optional, image upload (JPEG, PNG, WebP)
- Gender: required, pick one of Male, Female, Non-binary, Other, Prefer not to say
- Pronouns: optional, pick a suggestion (she/her, he/him, they/them, she/they, he/they) or type your own. Example: she/her
- Age range: optional, pick one of Under 18, 18–20, 21–24, 25–29, 30+. A range instead of a birth date, so we store less personal data.
- Nationality: optional, dropdown of all 249 countries with flags. Example: 🇦🇺 Australia
- Bio: optional, max 500 characters
- Filled in automatically, not asked: University (from the sign-up email) and Email (verified with a 6-digit code, never shown to other users)


========================================
CARD 2: ACADEMIC
========================================

CHECKLIST:
[BUILT] MVP · University (automatic, from sign-up email)
[BUILT] MVP · Department (required, free text; see decision 1)
[IDEA] MVP · Degree (required, dropdown of real degrees filtered by uni)
[IDEA] MVP · College / faculty (automatic, from degree)
[IDEA] MVP · Study level (Undergraduate / Honours / Postgraduate / Research; automatic from degree)
[BUILT] MVP · Year of study (optional, 1–10)
[IDEA] MVP · Domestic or international student (optional)
[IDEA] Nice · Major / specialisation (optional)
[IDEA] Nice · Current course codes (optional tags, e.g. COMP1010; for study buddies)

DESCRIPTION:
- University: automatic from the email. @adelaide.edu.au gives Adelaide University, @flinders.edu.au gives Flinders.
- Department: currently typed by hand
- Degree: searchable dropdown of real courses, taken from each uni's official course pages (checked 2026-09-26): 305 for Adelaide University and 368 for Flinders. The lists are in the degrees folder, and each course has its level, college, campuses and a link to its official page.
- College / faculty: filled in from the degree, so it's spelled the same for everyone and matching works
- Study level: filled in from the degree
- Year of study: number from 1 to 10
- Domestic / international: pick one. Helps international students find each other.
- Major: text or dropdown
- Course codes: tags. "Who else is in COMP1010?" makes a strong study-buddy feature.

CARD 3: WHAT THEY'RE LOOKING FOR
========================================

CHECKLIST:
[IDEA] MVP · Looking for (pick several: study buddy / gym buddy / friends / flatmate / language exchange / commute buddy / event buddy)
[IDEA] Nice · Buddy gender preference (any / same gender)

DESCRIPTION:
- Without "Looking for", the matching can only guess why someone joined. It's the most useful field we haven't built.

========================================
DECISIONS NEEDED FROM THE PM
========================================

- 1. Department vs degree: keep department as free text, or fill it in from the degree? Suggestion: fill it in from the degree, so it's spelled the same for everyone and "same department" matching works.
- 2. Nationality in matching: currently only shown on the profile. Options: ignore it, give same-country matches a small boost, or add a "show me people from my country" filter the user turns on.
- 3. Habits: free tags or fixed questions? See Card 4.
- 4. Age range vs date of birth: we store a range to hold less personal data. Is that enough?
- 5. Naming: Adelaide and UniSA are now Adelaide University, whose site is adelaide.edu.au. Should the app show "Adelaide University"?
- 6. Which courses go in the degree dropdown: all levels (bachelors, honours, masters, graduate certificates, PhDs), or just undergraduate and postgraduate coursework? The full lists have 673 courses; undergraduate only is 228.
- 7. Flinders college names: Flinders recently changed from 6 colleges to 5, and its course pages mostly still show the old names. Suggestion: use the old names for the demo, since that's what the course pages show.
